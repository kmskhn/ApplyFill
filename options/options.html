ApplyFill

Fills job forms from this profile, your saved answers and your resume. It never submits.

Get started
  1. AI tab: add a free Gemini key.
  2. Resume tab: upload your PDF, then click "Read text from PDF with AI". It also fills the empty fields below.
  3. Check this page, fill in the rest, and click Save.
  4. Open a job's apply page and click ⚡ Fill (or press Alt+Shift+F).

Gemini key (free)

Get one at aistudio.google.com/apikey. No card needed. If you already use Gemini for other apps, create the key in a new project: free limits are per project.

OpenRouter key (optional fallback)

Free models, about 50 requests a day per account (shared by all your apps on that account). Get one at openrouter.ai/keys.
